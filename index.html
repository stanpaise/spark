💡 Why Spark is different

⚡ Free & complete — no subscription, no paywall, no “pro” nagging.
📦 Local-first — your habits live on your device; no cloud account, no sync surprises, no duplicates.
👥 Accounts on this device — separate spaces for family & friends, each with its own streaks.
🗃 One file for everything — a whole-device backup moves every account to a new browser.
🚫 Updates never reorder or duplicate your habits — stable IDs and explicit ordering.
📔 A real journal — all notes across habits & dates in one searchable place.
❄️ Streak freezes — hit a 7-day milestone, bank a shield, and one missed day won't kill your streak.
🏠 Household board — everyone on this device, streaks side by side.
🗄 Archive, don't delete — park habits you've outgrown; their history stays. 📊 CSV export for spreadsheets.
🎉 Fun by design — streak flames, XP, confetti and awards keep you coming back.
